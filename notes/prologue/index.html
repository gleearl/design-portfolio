Prologue

Oct 22, 2026

Hi, I'm Glee. I'm based in Cebu, Philippines, and I've been working in tech for a while now. Long enough to know a few things, and long enough to know I still have so much to learn.

This is my first note here. Before I write about design or work, I figured I should introduce myself properly. Not the résumé version. The real one.

Coffee first

I love coffee, specialty coffee in particular. Lately I've been really enjoying filter coffee. It's slow, it feels like a small ritual, and every cup tastes a little different.

I'm also very, very close to buying a coffee machine for home. Like, one more good cup away from checking out. I'll let you know.

Outside

I love the outdoors. I hike, I camp overnight, and I cook outside whenever I get the chance. Food just tastes better at a campsite. Also, I love to eat, which probably explains the cooking.

And I love to travel. Most of my trips end up being about a trail, a mountain or a good place to eat.

The Tomorrowland thing

I love EDM, and right now I'm very excited because I'm going to my first ever Tomorrowland. It's happening in Thailand, and it's the first one there too.

I've been dreaming about Tomorrowland since college. I remember thinking, whoa, where is this place? The huge production, the stages, the famous DJs. And I remember wondering if there would ever be a time I'd actually get to go.

Ten, eleven years later, here I am. Going.

It may not be the holy grounds in Belgium, but Tomorrowland is still Tomorrowland, even in Thailand. It has six stages, so it's pretty much the same as Tomorrowland Brasil. College me would not believe this.

Almost 30

I'm turning 30 next year, and for me that's a bit crazy. I'm still figuring myself out. But I have goals, and I'm working toward them.

Work, so far

In my career so far, I'm really happy I landed at a company I'm still excited to work for, with people who share the same passion.

And the best thing about my work is that I'm still learning. I have lots to learn, and I welcome those challenges.

So, this is the prologue

I'm not totally sure where these notes will go yet. Some will be about design and how I work, some about whatever I'm learning at the moment, and probably a few about coffee. This is just the beginning.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
